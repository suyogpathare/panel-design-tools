    Validation record

    Record the specificity evidence you have for each antibody in the panel, following the five validation strategies proposed by the International Working Group for Antibody Validation (Uhlén M et al., Nature Methods 2016). Published knockout results and OMAP use are filled in where the public sources have them. The record lists evidence; it does not judge whether an antibody is validated.

    Add RRID

    Catalog data: Antibody Registry (RRIDs; CC BY, with vendor-supplied fields for non-commercial use, as here). Registry citation counts are as recorded by the registry and can be incomplete. Papers: number of articles in Europe PMC whose text mentions the clone name together with the target; a rough measure of use that can include unrelated mentions of the same name. Gene names, synonyms and HPA antibody reliability: Human Protein Atlas, CC BY 4.0 (Uhlén M et al., Tissue-based map of the human proteome, Science 2015). OMAP badges: antibodies used in the HuBMAP Organ Mapping Antibody Panels for multiplexed tissue imaging (Human Reference Atlas, CC BY 4.0), matched by RRID, or by clone and gene. KO badges: knockout-based characterization by YCharOS, published by Only Good Antibodies (CC BY 4.0), matched by RRID, or by gene and clone for the same monoclonal sold under another catalog number. ✓ supportive, ~ limited support, ✗ not supportive, in IHC or else cell immunofluorescence; hover for western blot and IP. Coverage is still small: 166 proteins as of November 2026, mostly from neuroscience. Validation record: strategies from Uhlén M et al., A proposal for validation of antibodies, Nature Methods 2016; results are what the user enters, and nothing is sent anywhere. Bleed-through estimates: dye spectra from FPbase (CC BY-SA 4.0; Lambert TJ, Nature Methods 2019). This page lists what the public sources record; check validation data with the vendor before use.
