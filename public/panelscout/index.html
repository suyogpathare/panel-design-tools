PanelScout

Paste a gene list to see how much of it each pre-designed imaging spatial transcriptomics panel covers, which genes each panel is missing, and which genes no panel has. Uses the gene lists 10x Genomics and Bruker publish for Xenium and CosMx. Protein and common names such as PD-L1 or Ki-67 are matched to gene symbols.

Gene lists, as published by each vendor (—):

    Gene symbols and aliases: HGNC (human) and MGI (mouse). Vendor names that have since been renamed are matched to the current symbol. Probes written as several genes (for example HBA1/2) count for each of those genes and are marked as shared.

    Not included yet: Vizgen MERSCOPE (gene lists are shared after registration) and Visium HD (its probe set covers most of the protein-coding transcriptome).
